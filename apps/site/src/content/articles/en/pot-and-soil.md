---

title: Choosing the right pot and soil
category: basics
---

A plant spends its whole life in its pot, so it's worth choosing it well. Three questions are enough.

## Always a hole in the bottom

Without a drainage hole, water builds up at the bottom and the roots rot. If you love a decorative outer pot, keep the plant in a pot with holes inside it, and empty the outer pot after each watering.

## The right size

A pot that's too big holds water for too long around roots that can't drink it. When repotting, pick a pot 2 to 4 cm wider than the old one, no more.

## The right material

- **Terracotta**: it breathes and dries quickly. Ideal for cacti, succulents and anyone with a heavy hand on the watering can.
- **Plastic**: light, it holds moisture longer. Handy for plants that like to stay moist, such as ferns.
- **Glazed ceramic**: pretty and heavy, it behaves like plastic.

## The soil

A general houseplant potting mix suits most indoor plants. Lighten it with a third of perlite or pumice for plants that dislike moisture. Cacti and succulents prefer a special, very free-draining mix, and orchids a bark-based one.

---

title: Plants for dark rooms
category: light
---

No plant lives without light, but some make do with very little. They're perfect for a north-facing room, a hallway or the back of a living room.

## The most tolerant

- **ZZ plant**: almost indestructible, it copes with dim light and forgotten waterings.
- **Snake plant**: it grows slowly, but lasts for years away from the window.
- **Cast-iron plant**: true to its name, it takes shade, cold and neglect.
- **Pothos**: it keeps its leaves green in low light, only its variegation fades.
- **Heartleaf philodendron**: easy-going, it handles modest light well.
- **Ferns**: many prefer soft light, as long as the air is humid enough.

## Good habits

- **Water less**: with little light, the plant grows slowly and drinks little.
- **Dust the leaves**: dust blocks light that is already scarce.
- **Move them closer to the window in winter**, when the sun is low and days are short.

## The limits

A plant that tolerates shade doesn't grow in the dark. If it stretches, turns pale or stops putting out new leaves, it needs more light. A grow light can then step in.

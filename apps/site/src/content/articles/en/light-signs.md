---

title: Too much or too little light?
category: light
---

A plant tells you quite clearly when it's in the wrong spot. You just need to read its leaves.

## Not enough light

- **It stretches**: stems get longer and leaves grow further apart. This is called etiolation.
- **It leans** heavily towards the window.
- **Its leaves turn pale** or the white and yellow variegation fades.
- **New leaves are smaller** than the old ones.
- **It stops flowering**, or drops its lower leaves.

Move it gradually closer to the window, and give the pot a quarter turn every week so it grows straight.

## Too much light

- **Dry brown or white patches** on the most exposed leaves: these are burns.
- **Leaves yellowing or bleaching** on the side facing the glass.
- **Leaves curling up** to protect themselves.
- **Soil that dries out in a single day.**

Move the plant back a metre or two, or filter the sun with a sheer curtain. Burnt leaves won't heal, but new ones will grow healthy.

## Moving a plant gently

A plant used to shade burns if it's suddenly put in full sun. Make the change over one to two weeks, increasing the exposure little by little.

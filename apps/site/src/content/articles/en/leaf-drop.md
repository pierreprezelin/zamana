---

title: My plant is dropping its leaves
category: health
---

A plant dropping its leaves is almost always reacting to a change or to stress. To find the cause, ask yourself what has changed over the last few weeks.

## It has just been moved

It's the number one cause, especially with ficus, which are very sensitive. A new spot changes the light, the temperature and the humidity. The plant drops a few leaves while it adapts. Leave it in its new place and avoid moving it again.

## It's cold, or there's a draught

An open window in winter, a front door, cold glass: many tropical plants drop their leaves below about 12 °C.

## Watering has changed

Soil that's too dry makes dry leaves fall. Soggy soil makes soft, yellow leaves fall. Check the soil with your finger before doing anything.

## It's the season

Some plants lose part of their foliage in autumn or winter, when light gets scarce. That's normal if new leaves grow back in spring.

## Pests

Look under the leaves: spider mites, mealybugs or thrips can make leaves fall.

In every case, avoid making several changes at once: a stressed plant mostly needs stability.

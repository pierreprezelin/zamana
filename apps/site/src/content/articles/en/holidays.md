---

title: Going on holiday without losing your plants
category: basics
---

A week away is no problem for most plants. For longer, a few precautions are enough to find them in good shape.

## Before you leave

- **Water thoroughly** the day before, until water runs out of the bottom.
- **Move plants away from the sunniest windows**: with less light, they drink less.
- **Group them** in a cool room: they'll hold moisture better.
- **Don't fertilize** or repot right before leaving.

## For two weeks or more

- **The wick**: a cotton cord links a bowl of water to the soil of each pot, and the plant drinks at its own pace.
- **Watering spikes** pushed into the soil, attached to an upturned bottle.
- **The bathtub**: set the pots on a thick, soaked towel at the bottom of a bathtub, with a little light.

Test your setup a few days before leaving, to make sure it doesn't drown the plants.

## The champions of absence

Cacti, succulents, ZZ plants and snake plants easily go three weeks without water. Above all, don't water them more than usual before you leave.

The simplest option is still to give a key to a neighbour, with a list of the plants to water.

---

title: Choosing a grow light
category: light
---

When the window isn't enough, in winter or in a dark room, a grow light gives the plant the light it's missing.

## Which light

- **LEDs**: they run cool, use little power and last a long time.
- **Full spectrum**, with white light: it suits every plant and stays pleasant in a living room, unlike pink or purple lights.
- **A screw-in bulb** is enough for one or two plants. For a whole shelf, prefer a light bar or a panel.

A regular household bulb isn't strong enough to grow a plant.

## Setting it up

- **Distance**: 20 to 50 cm above the leaves, depending on the power. Too close, the leaves burn; too far, the light barely helps.
- **Duration**: 10 to 14 hours a day. Plants need night too.
- **A timer** switches the light on and off at fixed times, without you having to think about it.

## Signs it's working

The plant puts out new leaves that are bigger and more colourful, and stops stretching. If pale or burnt patches appear, move the light a little further away.

---

title: Brown leaf tips and edges
category: health
---

Dry brown tips at the end of the leaves are one of the most common houseplant problems, especially in palms, dracaenas, calatheas and spider plants.

## Possible causes

- **The air is too dry**, especially in winter with the heating on. It's the most frequent cause.
- **Watering is irregular**: the soil swings from soggy to bone dry.
- **The water is too hard or chlorinated**, which bothers sensitive plants such as calatheas and dracaenas.
- **Too much fertilizer**: salts build up and burn the tips of the roots, then of the leaves.
- **A draught**, cold or hot, near a door or a radiator.

## What to do

1. **Trim the brown parts** with clean scissors, following the shape of the leaf and leaving a thin brown edge so you don't cut into the green.
2. **Raise the humidity** around the plant.
3. **Water more regularly**, with water that has stood overnight or rainwater.
4. **Flush the soil** with plenty of water if you suspect too much fertilizer.

Tips that are already brown won't turn green again, but if new leaves stay healthy, the problem is solved.

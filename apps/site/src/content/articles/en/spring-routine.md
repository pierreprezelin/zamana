---

title: The spring routine
category: seasons
---

In spring, days get longer and plants wake up. It's the best time to give them a little health check.

## The checklist

- **Repot** those that are running out of room: roots coming out of the pot, water running straight through.
- **Resume fertilizer**, at a low dose at first, as soon as new leaves appear.
- **Water a little more, gradually**: with more light and warmth, the plant drinks more.
- **Prune** bare or overly long stems, for a bushier plant. It's also the ideal time to take cuttings.
- **Dust the leaves** with a damp cloth, so they make the most of the light.
- **Check under the leaves**: aphids come back with the first warm days.

## Mind the sun

Spring sunshine gets strong quickly. A plant that spent the winter away from the window can burn if it's suddenly put back in full sun. Make the change over one to two weeks.

## And outdoors?

Wait until nights no longer drop below 12 to 15 °C before taking houseplants outside. Late frosts can still strike in mid-spring.

_In the southern hemisphere, spring runs from September to November._

---

title: Bottom watering
category: watering
---

Instead of pouring water onto the soil, you can let the plant drink from the bottom of the pot. It's a simple method, ideal for some plants.

## How to do it

1. Fill a basin or a sink with a few centimetres of room-temperature water.
2. Set the pot in it: it must have a drainage hole.
3. Let the plant drink for 15 to 30 minutes, until the top of the soil is moist.
4. Lift the pot out and let it drain before putting it back.

## Its advantages

- **The whole root ball gets wet**, even if it was very dry and water used to run off it.
- **The leaves and the base of the stem stay dry**, which prevents rot in sensitive plants.
- **The roots grow down** towards the bottom of the pot to reach the water.

It's the favourite method for African violets, cyclamens and rosette succulents.

## Its limits

Bottom watering never flushes the soil: salts from the water and the fertilizer build up on top. Once a month, water normally from above, until water runs out of the bottom.

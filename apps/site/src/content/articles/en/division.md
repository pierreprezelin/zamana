---

title: Dividing a plant into several
category: propagation
---

Some plants grow in clumps, with several stems coming up from the soil. You can split them into two or three plants, each with its own roots. It's the fastest way to get good-sized plants.

## Plants that can be divided

Snake plants, ZZ plants, calatheas, ferns, cast-iron plants, peace lilies, spider plants and most clump-forming herbs.

## The right time

In spring, when you repot. The plant has the whole season to recover.

## Step by step

1. **Water the day before**, then take the plant out of its pot.
2. **Remove the soil** around the roots to spot the different clumps.
3. **Pull them apart** gently by hand. If the roots are too tangled, slice through with a clean knife.
4. **Keep roots and leaves** on each piece.
5. **Pot each division** in a pot that fits it, with fresh potting mix.
6. **Water**, then keep them out of direct sun for a week.

Divisions may look tired for a few days while they grow new roots. No fertilizer for a month.

## Offsets

Spider plants, aloes and bromeliads produce babies, called offsets or pups. Once they have their own roots, detach them and plant them in a small pot.

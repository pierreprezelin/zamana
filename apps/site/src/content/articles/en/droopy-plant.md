---

title: My plant is limp and drooping
category: health
---

Hanging leaves and sagging stems are always worrying. The good news: the cause is almost always in the soil.

## The soil is dry

The plant is simply thirsty. Water thoroughly, ideally from the bottom if the soil is very dry and water runs off it. Most plants perk up within a few hours.

## The soil is soggy

This is more serious: suffocated roots can no longer drink, and the plant wilts even though it has water. Above all, don't water more. Empty the saucer, let it dry out, and if nothing changes within a few days, take the plant out of its pot to check the roots.

## It's very hot

In the heat, some plants shut down in the middle of the day to save water, then perk up in the evening. If so, move them to the shade rather than watering them again.

## It has just been repotted

Repotting always damages the roots a little. A plant may stay limp for a few days: keep it out of direct sun and water sparingly.

## The stem is black or soft at the base

That's a sign of stem rot. Take a healthy cutting while there's still time.

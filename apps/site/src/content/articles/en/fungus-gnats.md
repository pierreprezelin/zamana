---

title: Those little flies in the soil
category: health
---

The small black flies buzzing around your pots are fungus gnats. The adults are mostly annoying, but their larvae live in the soil and can damage the roots of young plants.

## Why they're there

They lay their eggs in soil that stays moist on top. Their presence is almost always a sign of too much watering.

## Getting rid of them

1. **Let the soil dry out** several centimetres down between waterings: the larvae die without moisture.
2. **Water from the bottom** for a few weeks, to keep the surface dry.
3. **Put yellow sticky traps** near the pots, to catch the adults and break the egg-laying cycle.
4. **Cover the soil** with a thin layer of sand or gravel, where females can no longer lay eggs.

For a large infestation, garden centres sell nematodes, which eat the larvae without harming the plant.

## Preventing them

Don't keep an open, damp bag of potting mix indoors, empty the saucers, and only water when the top of the soil is dry.

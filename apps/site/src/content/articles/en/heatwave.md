---

title: Protecting your plants from a heatwave
category: seasons
---

Above 30 to 35 °C, even heat-loving plants suffer. A few simple steps are enough to help them get through it. Zamana also warns you about strong heat for your outdoor plants.

## Indoors

- **Move plants away from south- and west-facing windows**: behind glass, leaves burn fast.
- **Close the shutters or curtains** during the hottest hours, as you would for yourself.
- **Water more often**, but always check the soil first: heat doesn't change the finger rule.

## Outdoors

- **Water early in the morning or in the evening**, at the base, never in full sun.
- **Move pots into the shade**, or cover them with shade cloth. Black and plastic pots heat up a lot: group them or shelter them.
- **Mulch the top of the soil**, with bark or wood chips, to keep it cool.
- **Put the pots on a saucer** during the heatwave only: a small water reserve helps them get through the afternoons.

## What to avoid

- **Fertilizer**: a plant suffering from the heat can't use it.
- **Repotting and pruning**, which add stress.
- **Misting in full sun**: droplets can burn the leaves.

A plant that wilts at midday and perks up in the evening is coping with the heat. If it's still limp in the morning, it needs water.

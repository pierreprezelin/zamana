---

title: Choosing a healthy plant
category: basics
---

A plant well chosen at the store is half the work done. A few minutes of inspection save a lot of disappointment.

## Before buying

- **Think about the spot**: a plant that loves direct light won't survive in a hallway. Choose it for your room, not the other way round.
- **Check whether it's toxic** if you have a cat, a dog or young children.

## At the store

- **The leaves**: well coloured, firm, with no spots, dry edges or holes.
- **Under the leaves and where they meet the stems**: no cottony traces, no webs, no moving dots. That's where pests hide.
- **The soil**: neither soggy nor smelly, with no flies taking off.
- **The roots**: a few roots coming out of the bottom are normal, but a pot completely full of roots will need repotting soon.
- **The shape**: a compact, bushy plant rather than a stretched one, a sign it has had enough light.
- **Flowering plants**: pick them with lots of buds and few open flowers, so the blooms last longer at home.

## Once home

Keep the newcomer away from your other plants for two to three weeks, to make sure it hasn't brought pests along. Wait before repotting it too: it first needs to get used to its new surroundings.

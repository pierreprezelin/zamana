---

title: Getting rid of aphids
category: health
---

Aphids are small green, black or white insects that gather in colonies on young shoots and flower buds. They multiply very fast, especially in spring.

## Spotting them

- Clusters of small insects on tender stems and under young leaves.
- Distorted, curled or sticky leaves: that's the honeydew they give off.
- Ants climbing the plant, drawn by this honeydew.

## Treating them

1. **Squash or remove them** by hand, or cut off the most infested shoots.
2. **Shower the plant** with a jet of lukewarm water, covering the soil.
3. **Spray diluted soft soap** (one tablespoon per litre of lukewarm water) over the whole plant and under the leaves.
4. **Repeat** every three or four days until they're gone.

Outdoors, ladybirds and their larvae are fearsome aphid eaters: let them do their job.

## Preventing them

Aphids love plants full of nitrogen fertilizer, with soft new growth. Stick to the fertilizer doses, and check plants you bring in from the garden.

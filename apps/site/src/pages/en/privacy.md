Zamana is a free app, with no ads and **no account**. There's nothing to sign up for or fill in to use it, and no data is sold or used for advertising.

The data controller is Pierre Prézelin, who you can reach at [contact@pierreprezelin.com](mailto:contact@pierreprezelin.com).

## What stays on your phone

Your plants, their photos, their care, their history, your first name if you enter it and your settings are stored only on your phone. They're not sent anywhere and we have no access to them. One exception: if your phone backup is turned on (iCloud, Google), the system may include them.

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled by the app itself: no server is needed to get them.

## What is sent, and why

A few pieces of data leave your phone to make the app work:

- **Open-Meteo**, for weather alerts: the city you search for in the settings and, only if you have outdoor plants, your location rounded to about 10 km and your time zone for the forecast. See [Open-Meteo's privacy policy](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms#privacy).
- **iNaturalist**, for the plant encyclopedia: the text of your plant searches, and the app language and version. Popular plants load as soon as you open the encyclopedia, and the photos of the plants shown or added from it are served by Amazon Web Services for iNaturalist. No account or location is sent. See [iNaturalist's privacy policy](https://www.inaturalist.org/pages/privacy).
- **Your phone's location service** (Apple or Google), if you choose "My location": your rounded location, to show the name of the matching city.
- **The Zamana API**, hosted by Vercel, to check that your version of the app is up to date: your IP address and the technical details of the connection. The app compares its version with the required minimum itself. Vercel keeps these technical logs for one hour. See [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- **The zamana.garden website**, hosted by Cloudflare, to download the help articles: your IP address and the app language. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
- **Expo Observe**, to measure performance and fix bugs: loading times, errors and crashes, battery and network state at launch, phone model, system and app versions, and a random installation ID. It isn't linked to your name or your plants. This data is kept for 90 days. See [Expo's privacy policy](https://expo.dev/privacy).

As with any exchange over the internet, these services also see the IP address of your connection.

## Permissions

The app only asks for a permission when it needs it, and you can revoke it at any time in your phone settings:

- **Notifications**: for your care reminders and weather alerts.
- **Approximate location**: for weather alerts where you are. You can choose a city instead.
- **Camera and photos**: to show your plants. The photos you pick are copied into the app, downsized and stripped of their metadata (like where they were taken), and stay on your phone.

## Retention and your rights

Data stored on your phone stays there until you delete it. Uninstalling the app erases all of it from the phone.

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or erase your data by writing to [contact@pierreprezelin.com](mailto:contact@pierreprezelin.com). You can also lodge a complaint with the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/en/complaints), the French data protection authority, or the one in your country.

## This website

The zamana.garden website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. Only your choice of light or dark theme is remembered, in your browser.

# Zamana

Your personal reminder about how and when to take care of your precious plants 🪴

## About

Like many, I love my plants, am concerned when they are down, and sad when they die. But as their number grew, I had a harder time remembering what each one of them needs to thrive.

Light or shade? Temperature range? Keep their roots in water or not?

I created _Zamana_ to be my assistant whenever I'm in doubt about what to do.

## _Zamana?_

I lived on Martinique Island for quite some time, and there is a gigantic, 300-year-old "Zamana" (_Samanea saman_) tree in L'Habitation Céron, an ancient sugar plantation from the 1700s. I thought it would be a nice tribute to this magnificent "rain tree", which covers more than 5,000 square meters with its branches and was awarded France's Tree of the Year in 2016.

If you ever stop by, make sure to pay it a visit!

## Features

I designed Zamana to cover my needs, but also to be useful to everyone. You'll find:

- 🗒️ Fully customizable info about your plant (name, temp, watering, lifespan...)
- 🌡️ Notifications about abnormally hot or cold temperatures, to bring your plants indoors
- ⚠️ Alerts to remind you to take care of your plants at specific intervals
- 🖼️ A timeline with the photos you've taken of your plant to watch its evolution
- 📍 Everything stored locally on your phone...
- 💾 ...with maybe an optional account in the future, if you wish to sync it with the cloud?
- 🌍 Translated in English and French (for now)
- 💎 No subscription, no paid tier, no ads
- 🍎 Available on Android and iOS

**Feel free to give suggestions for a better experience!**
